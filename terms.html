Terms

This site belongs to a private fantasy football league of about thirty friends. It is not a business, nothing is sold here, and there is no money involved in using it. The league's own prize money is settled between the managers and has nothing to do with this website.

Who it is for

The Subs Room is for managers in the Football Friends league. Signing in requires a Fantrax Secret ID belonging to a team in this league, so nobody else can use it.

What you agree to

  • Set substitutes for your own team, not anybody else's.
  • What you set before a deadline stands. After the deadline it cannot be changed, by you or by anyone else.
  • The league's own rules decide what happens with those substitutes. They are on the Rulebook page.

What we promise, and what we do not

This is a hobby project run by the commissioner. It is offered as it is, with no guarantee that it will be available at any given moment. If it is down when you want to set your subs, tell the group and the commissioner will sort it out.

Substitutions are applied by the commissioner after the gameweek, following the published rules. Mistakes get fixed; arguments are settled the way they always have been.

Ending it

Ask the commissioner and your sign-in and your saved orders are deleted. Leaving the league means leaving this too.
