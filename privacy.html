Privacy

Football Friends is a private fantasy league of about thirty friends. The site is a record of that league. One page on it, the Subs Room, asks you to sign in. This explains what that does with your information.

What is stored when you sign in

  • Your email address and Google account identifier, so we know it is you next time.
  • Which team is yours, and the substitute order you set for a gameweek.

That is the whole list.

The Fantrax Secret ID

The first time you sign in you are asked once for your Fantrax Secret ID. It is used immediately to ask Fantrax which team in this league belongs to you, and is then discarded. It is never written down, never logged, and never sent anywhere else. It is not a password and cannot be used to change anything in your Fantrax account.

Who can see it

Once a gameweek's deadline passes, the substitute order you set is shown to the rest of the league, which is the point of it. Your email address is not shown to anyone except the league commissioner.

Where it lives, and for how long

In a Cloudflare Workers KV store belonging to the commissioner. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising, and there is no analytics or tracking anywhere on this site.

Ask the commissioner and your sign-in record and any orders you have set will be deleted. If you simply stop using the page, they stay until you ask.

Changes

If this ever changes, it will change here, and the league will be told in the group.
